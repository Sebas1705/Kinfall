# Kinfall visual style: "dusk-lit pixel fantasy" — design

Status: phase A (shared art core and this spec) built on branch `visual-style`; phases B (web, Pixi) and C (mobile, Skia) consume it.
Date: 2026-11-20.

Legend: **[Owner]** = decided by the owner in conversation. **[My call]** = chosen by the designer under the owner's delegation; the owner may change it.

## 1. Why

The owner said the current UI feels like a mobile app, not a game, and asked for decoration, 2D designs, backgrounds and effects so it feels like a game. **[Owner]** The owner delegated the choice of style. **[Owner]**

What exists before this work: creatures are generated 24x24 pixel grids (`src/art/creatureGen.ts`) with one 6-colour palette per element, element icons are 8x8 grids, the web battle scene (Pixi) draws them on a flat two-colour background with flat HUD panels, and the mobile app draws them with Skia rects. Menus, maps and events on both apps are plain rounded cards.

## 2. The style

**Dusk-lit pixel fantasy.** **[My call]**

- **Crisp pixel art.** Integer scaling only, nearest-neighbour sampling, no smoothing, no sub-pixel positions. Every position the core hands out is a whole art pixel.
- **A limited dusk palette per zone,** with one warm light accent: the ember glow of a setting sun, firefly gold, the rose light of a veiled moon. Light comes from one side per zone and rims every silhouette on that side.
- **Layered parallax landscapes:** a banded sky (flat bands, ordered dither only at the seams), a sun or moon with a dithered halo, thin lit clouds, far silhouettes in haze, middle silhouettes (dead trees and a broken arch, thorn trees with hanging lanterns and mossy pillars, sea cliffs with ruined watchtowers), a textured floor with pools that reflect the sky, and a dark foreground fringe over the creatures.
- **A ground platform and a soft pixel shadow** under each creature (a floating disc of earth with a lit rim and tufts).
- **Small ambient life** per zone: rising embers and drifting ash, blinking fireflies and falling leaves, sea mist and sparkles.
- **Chunky pixel HUD plates and buttons** (nine-slice frames with a bevelled trim band and metal corner brackets set with a zone-coloured gem) instead of flat rounded cards.
- **Element-themed move effects** (ember sparks, thorn spikes and leaves, tide droplets and ripples, gale streaks and swirls, umbra wisps) and **event flourishes** (hit flash, screen shake, super-effective stars, resisted puff, heal motes, faint, evolve, recruit, level up, soul gain, victory, defeat), plus floating numbers.
- **A pixel wordmark** for the title, **node-map icons** and **small event vignettes**.
- **Text stays real text** rendered by the platform (accessible, translatable, scalable). The only letters baked into art are the decorative logo, which always carries an accessible label.
- **Everything is generated by code** (no image or font files), **deterministic from a seed**, and original: no traces of existing franchises (no pokeball-like shapes, no borrowed logos or sprites, our own letterforms).

### Approaches considered

| Option | What it would look like | Why not (or why) |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Dusk-lit pixel fantasy (chosen)** | Pixel scenes, dither, rim light, chunky frames | Matches the existing generated pixel creatures, stays crisp at any integer scale, cheap to draw on low-end phones, and pixel art reads as "game" immediately. Data is tiny (palette indices) and shared by both renderers. |
| B. Painted vector storybook | Soft gradients, SVG-like shapes, blur glows | Clashes with the 24x24 pixel creatures; gradients and blur cost more on phones and differ between Skia and the browser canvas. |
| C. Flat UI with a game skin | Keep cards, add icons and colours | Cheapest, but it is exactly what the owner rejected: it still feels like an app. |
| D. Hand-made sprite sheets | Imported PNGs | Breaks "everything generated by code", needs an artist and licences, and grows the bundle. |

## 4. Scene model and layers

Logical scene: **256x144 art pixels** (16:9), horizon at row 84. Renderers pick the largest whole-number scale that fits and may letterbox or extend the tiled layers sideways (every tiled layer is exactly 256 wide and wraps seamlessly).

| Layer (draw order) | Size | Parallax | Depth | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sky | 4 x 88, tiled | 0 | back | flat bands with a 4x4 ordered dither in the last 40% of each band |
| stars | 256 x 52, tiled | 0.02 | back | fewer in the marsh; twinkles under the moons |
| light | up to 63 x 63 | 0.04 | back | sun or moon disc, shaded limb or sinking lower half, dithered halo with alpha |
| clouds | 256 x 40, tiled | 0.08 | back | lit edge towards the light; drift 1.5 px/s (0 with reduced motion) |
| far | 256 x 40, tiled | 0.15 | back | hills, conifer ridge or sea stacks; haze rows at the bottom; rim light |
| mid | 256 x 78, tiled | 0.4 | back | the zone's landmark silhouettes, side rim light, warm detail pixels |
| floor | 256 x 60, tiled | 0.6 | back | dithered light near the horizon, darker near the viewer, patches, pools, specks, stones |
| near | 256 x 30, tiled | 1 | front | dark fringe, tall clumps only at the tile seam so the creatures stay clear |

Camera: a renderer may sway the camera a few pixels (the preview uses +-6 px over about 19 s) and shake it during effects; each layer moves by `-camera.x * parallax`. With reduced motion the camera stays still and clouds do not drift.

Stage (`STAGE`, `stageAnchors(side)`): the player's feet at (70, 122) on the larger `near` platform, the enemy's at (188, 100) on the `far` platform. Creatures are drawn at **2 art pixels per sprite cell** (48x48), bottom-centred on the feet point, the player mirrored to face right. The sprite's last row is empty, so sink it by one sprite row (`(height - 1) * 2`). Order: back layers, back ambient particles, platform, shadow (centred on the feet), creature, front layers, front ambient particles, effect particles, screen wash, then real-text titles and numbers.

Budget per backdrop: at most `SCENE_PIXEL_BUDGET` = 100,000 palette cells (about 81,000 today), one byte each.

## 5. Effects

Every effect is an `EffectDescriptor`: emitters (sprite mask, colours, count, start and spread, life range, origin, motion, speed or orbit radius, angle range, gravity, drag, size, fades, glow, blink) plus keyframe tracks applied to the target sprite (flash, offset, alpha, tint), the screen (shake, colour wash) and an optional real-text title (victory, defeat). Particle sprites are 1-colour white masks up to 7x7 (`particleSprites()`), tinted per particle. Effect particles are drawn at size 2 by default (the creatures' pixel density); ambient particles at size 1 (the backdrop's).

| Effect | ms | Particles | Reduced motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| `move.ember` | 760 | 22: 3 travelling embers, 14 rising sparks, 5 smoke motes | static frame at 350 ms for 300 ms |
| `move.thorn` | 760 | 16: 6 spikes bursting from the ground, 10 orbiting leaves | static |
| `move.tide` | 800 | 18: 4 arcing droplets, 12 splash droplets, 2 ripple rings | static |
| `move.gale` | 680 | 15: 7 wind streaks, 8 orbiting swirls | static |
| `move.umbra` | 840 | 20: 12 converging wisps, 8 popping motes; dark tint | static |
| `hit` | 280 | 5 sparks; 1 flash (90 ms), 3 px knockback, 1 px shake | static, no flash or shake |
| `superEffective` | 460 | 11: 10 stars and a ring; 2 flashes, 3 px shake | static |
| `resisted` | 380 | 4 dull motes; grey tint | static |
| `miss` | 320 | 3 streaks past the target; a small dodge | none |
| `heal` | 900 | 12 rising plus motes; green tint | static |
| `faint` | 900 | 10 dust motes; sinks 10 px and fades out | instant (gone at once) |
| `evolve` | 1600 | 36: 14 converging stars, 8 light beams, 14 sparks; 1 flash; gold tint | static |
| `recruit` | 1200 | 18: 12 orbiting motes, 6 stars; a hop | static |
| `levelUp` | 1000 | 12: 2 rings, 10 rising stars; a hop | static |
| `soulGain` | 900 | 8 blinking soul motes | static |
| `victory` | 1800 | 30: 16 bursting stars, 14 falling flakes; title rises in | static |
| `defeat` | 1800 | 16 falling ash flakes; screen dims to 55%; title fades in | instant (dim and title at once) |

`effectsForBattleEvent(event, content)` maps the sim's events: `move` (hit) to the move's element effect towards the opponent (or the user for self-targeted moves), `move` (miss) to `miss`, `damage` to `superEffective` (effectiveness >= 200), `resisted` (<= 50) or `hit`, `heal`, `fainted`, and `end` to `victory` or `defeat`. Run events (recruit, evolve, level, souls) are triggered by the screens that show them.

Floating numbers (`floatingNumberStyle`): damage, super effective (larger, pops), resisted (smaller rise), heal and soul (`+` prefix); fill, an 8-direction outline, a drop shadow, a rise and fade track. Real text, hidden from screen readers.

## 6. HUD frames and icons

- **Plate** (`plateFrame`): 16x16, slice 6, content inset 5/4. Dark outline, a 2-cell trim band lit top-left and shaded bottom-right, a dark inner line, the body, and L-shaped corner brackets with a 2x2 gem in the zone accent.
- **Button** (`buttonFrame(state)`): 14x14, slice 5; `normal` is raised (light top edge, two shade rows at the bottom, tiny gems), `pressed` sinks (shade on top, content one cell lower), `disabled` is flat, `focused` adds a 2-cell ring in the focus colour separated by a 1-cell gap in the page colour (20x20, slice 8). The platform keeps its own focus handling; the ring is the visual.
- **Bars** (`barStyle('hp' | 'focus')`): an 8x7 track frame (slice 3) and fill colours. HP above 50% high, above 25% mid, else low (`barFill`); a bright top row on the fill; HP tick marks every 25%. The value is always written next to the bar in text.
- **Node icons** (`nodeIcon`): 16x16, outlined, one distinct silhouette each: claw slashes (battle), spiked crest (elite), horned mask (boss), paw with a sparkle (recruit), standing stone with a rune (shrine), banded chest (cache), mist spiral (mystery). `iconForNodeType` maps the run's node types. The map keeps naming nodes in text.
- **Event vignettes** (`eventVignette(kind, zone, seed)`): 64x40 scenes for shrine, cache, mystery and recruit camp, lit by the zone's sky.
- **Wordmark** (`wordmark()`): the KINFALL letters in an original 9-row, 2-cell-stroke face, three-band ember fill with dithered seams, outline, 2-cell drop shadow and a falling star over the I. 68x19 cells; use scale 2 on phones and 3 on wide screens. Expose it as an image named "Kinfall" (`labelKey: 'app.title'`).

## 7. Motion rules

- One orchestrated moment per action: a move effect, then the event effect (hit, faint...), then numbers. Effects never overlap more than a move plus an event.
- Durations stay at or under 2 s; menus never wait on decorative motion longer than the current battle animator already does.
- Flashes: at most 2 pulses per second (tested), never full-screen white. The web app's existing 500 ms flash gap still applies on top.
- Shake: at most 3 art pixels, under 300 ms.
- Ambient life is slow (under 20 px/s) and never crosses the HUD plates' text (they sit outside the scene or over it with an opaque body).

## 8. Accessibility rules

- **Reduced motion:** every effect has a `reduced` rule (`static`, `instant` or `none`); `sampleEffect` applies it when `reducedMotion` is true (no shake, no flash, no travel, frozen particles, short hold). Ambient life freezes with fewer particles, clouds stop, the camera stays still. Both apps already have a reduce-motion setting (web toggle plus `prefers-reduced-motion`, mobile system setting): pass it through.
- **Contrast:** AA (4.5:1) for every text-bearing surface, 3:1 for the focus ring and meter fills; checked in `packages/core/tests/art/scene.test.ts`.
- **Decorative art is hidden** from screen readers: backdrops, platforms, particles, frames, icons, vignettes and floating numbers (`aria-hidden` / `importantForAccessibility="no-hide-descendants"`). Everything they show is also in text (names, HP, the battle log, node labels, event text). The only labelled art is the logo.
- **Never colour alone:** icons have distinct silhouettes (tested), HP thresholds are also numbers.
- **Text is never in art** except the logo; it scales with the text-size setting and the system font scale.

## 9. Performance budget (low-end phones)

- Backdrop: at most 100,000 palette cells per zone; bake each layer once into a texture (web) or a picture/image (Skia); re-bake only on zone change.
- Particles: at most 40 per effect (`EFFECT_PARTICLE_CAP`), 32 ambient (`AMBIENT_PARTICLE_CAP`), 112 on screen (`SCREEN_PARTICLE_CAP`); glowing particles add one dim halo each.
- Samplers are stateless and allocation-light: `prepareEffect` / `prepareAmbient` once, then `sampleEffect` / `sampleAmbient` per frame.
- Mobile: if a frame takes too long, drop ambient life first, then glows; never drop the effects' meaning (flash, tint, numbers).

## 10. Determinism and independence from the simulation

- Art never uses the simulation's PRNG. `cosmeticRng` (mulberry32 with an art-only FNV salt) is separate; a test checks no scene file imports `sim/rng` and that generating art does not change a sim stream. Battle and run goldens are untouched.
- Art uses only `+ - * /` and `Math.floor/min/max/abs/imul` (`fixedMath.ts` provides sine, square root and rounding from those), so the determinism guard passes and a seed draws the same pixels on V8 and Hermes.
- `packages/core/tests/golden/art.golden.json` pins 204 hashes (backdrop layers, platforms, ambient samples, vignettes, effects and their samples, icons, sprites, logo, frames, numbers) for two seeds. Regenerate it with `npm run golden:art` when the art changes on purpose, review the preview, and commit it with the change. It is separate from the battle and run goldens.
- Seed choice: use the run seed for the zone scene so a run always looks the same; effects can use any stable key (for example the run seed plus the turn number).

## 11. Contract for phases B (web, Pixi) and C (mobile, Skia)

Import everything from `@kinfall/core/art/scene`.

| Need | Call | Draw it as |
|---|---|---|
| A battle scene | `sceneForZone(zoneIndex, runSeed)` (cache it) | `backdrop.layers` in order: bake each `grid` into a nearest-neighbour texture/image; place at `(x + shift, y)`; tile `tileX` layers across the width; `depth: 'front'` after the creatures |
| Platforms and shadows | `scene.platforms.player / enemy`, `stageAnchors(side)` | platform at `feet - anchor`, shadow centred on `feet` |
| Creatures | existing `generateCreature` | at `STAGE.spriteScale` (2), bottom-centred on `feet`, player mirrored |
| Ambient life | `prepareAmbient(scene.ambient, runSeed)`, then `sampleAmbient(prep, tMs, reducedMotion)` per frame | each `DrawParticle`: the tinted `particleSprites()[sprite]` mask at `(x, y)` centred, scaled by `size`, opacity `alpha`; `depth` decides before or after the creatures |
| A battle event | `effectsForBattleEvent(event, CONTENT)` | for each: `prepareEffect(effectFor(id), key)`, then each frame `sampleEffect(prep, { tMs, target, ground, source, facing, reducedMotion })` until `done` |
| Effect frame | the `EffectFrame` | particles as above; `flash` = white silhouette of the target at that opacity; `tint` = tinted silhouette; `offset` and `alpha` move and fade the target sprite; `shake` offsets the whole stage; `screen` = full-scene colour wash; `title` = opacity and y offset for a real-text title |
| Floating numbers | `floatingNumberStyle(kind)` | real text with an 8-way outline and shadow, moved by `offsetY`, `alpha`, `scale` keyframes (`keyAt`); hidden from screen readers |
| HUD plates, buttons, bars | `plateFrame`, `buttonFrame(state)`, `barStyle(kind)` with `{ theme, zone }` | nine-slice at a whole-number scale (web: CSS `border-image` or Pixi `NineSliceSprite`; Skia: draw the 9 regions); text inside `content` in `textColor`; `barFill(style, percent)` |
| Map and events | `nodeIcon(iconForNodeType(type))`, `eventVignette(kind, zone, runSeed)` | crisp at 2x-3x, decorative |
| Title | `wordmark()` | at `scales[0]` (phones) or `scales[1]` (wide), labelled with `t('app.title')` |
| Skia rects | existing `gridToRects(grid)` | works for every grid here (backdrop layers are bigger: prefer baking them into an image once) |

Rules for both renderers: whole-number scales only, never smooth, never tint the backdrop for the UI theme, pass the reduce-motion flag to every sampler, keep everything decorative out of the accessibility tree, and keep new art out of the web app's initial bundle (load it with the battle chunk).

## 12. Preview

`npm run dev`, then open `/art-preview.html` (dev only: it is not a Vite build input, so the production bundle is unchanged). It shows every zone scene with parallax sway, platforms, ambient life and all effects cycling, an effect gallery, the frames as real DOM buttons and plates, icons at 1x-3x, vignettes, floating numbers and the logo, on dusk and parchment. Query parameters: `seed`, `scale`, `zone`, `only` (`scenes`, `effects`, `frames`, `icons`, `vignettes`, `logo`, `numbers`) and `at` (freeze the clock in ms, for screenshots). The page's own reduce-motion checkbox follows `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 13. Phase B (web) as built

- **Battle** (`apps/web/src/view/battleView.ts`, Pixi): one `TilingSprite` per tiled backdrop layer, sprites for the light, platforms, shadows and creatures, pooled tinted sprites for particles, Pixi text for numbers and the result title. Effects are started by the battle events inside the existing animator but never lengthen it: they run on the scene clock and may outlive the turn (the turn timings and the e2e contracts are unchanged). Hit flashes keep the 500 ms gap. The HUD is two DOM plates (`aria-hidden`), stacked above and below the scene on phones and floating over the sky from 48rem. Scale: the largest whole number that shows the whole scene, or (on phones) at least x 46..212 centred on the creatures, with the top 26 sky rows cropped on narrow or short windows; the canvas runs edge to edge on phones.
- **Screens** (`apps/web/src/art/screenArt.ts`, one lazy chunk that installs itself): title hero (zone 0 with the chosen starter on the near platform and an unknown silhouette on the far one) with the wordmark as `role="img"` named by `app.title`, map banner and node icons (into the map's icon slot), event vignettes, end scene with the victory/defeat flourish. Canvas 2D through `apps/web/src/art/canvas2d.ts` (shared with the dev preview); one 30 fps loop for all visible scenes, paused when hidden, off with reduced motion.
- **Frames** (`apps/web/src/art/frames.ts`, `art.css`): border-image custom properties for the current scheme (dusk/parchment) and zone; buttons raised, pressed on `:active`, flat when disabled, and the focus frame drawn outside the box (border-image outset) so focus never shifts layout; the main action wears the plate. The page palette follows the UI palettes.
- **Motion**: ambient frames at 30 fps, effects at the display rate; measured in Edge at 1280x800 with a 6x CPU throttle: 30 scene fps idle, about 46 during a turn, worst frame 33 ms.

## 14. Not done in phase A

- No change to the game screens in phase A (phase B above; phase C is mobile).
- No sound.
- Contrast is checked numerically; the effects have not been reviewed by someone with photosensitivity or vestibular needs.
- Hermes equality of the art is designed (exact maths only) but not run on a device; the art golden runs in Node.
